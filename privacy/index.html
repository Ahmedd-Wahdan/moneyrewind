Privacy Policy

Money Rewind Studio · last updated October 25, 2026

What the app accesses

When the account owner connects their TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (display name). It uses them only to upload the owner's own videos to that account and to show which account is connected.

Where data is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database, and it does not collect information about any other TikTok users or viewers.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone. Data is sent only to TikTok, as needed to upload videos.

Deleting data

Disconnecting the account in the app deletes its tokens. Access can also be revoked in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions.

Contact

Contact the creator through the TikTok account @moneyrewind.
